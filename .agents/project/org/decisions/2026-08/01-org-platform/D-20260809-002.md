## Options considered
1. Keep the ad-hoc backtest reporting (Sharpe/maxDD only, no significance test,
   no audit gate) — rejected: every strategy can claim "beats SPY" on luck.
2. House backtest v2: a chi-square gate (regime×win/loss), the full metric
   bundle (Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar, win-rate, FF5 alpha + CI, excess, fees,
   trades), the audit-status line, fixed windows, a dedicated backtest-agent on
   Gemini 3.1 Pro, and an archetype council whose voices enter every T3
   synthesis.

## Positions
- **big-pickle:** The gate must be fixed-calendar and falsifiable: pre-registered
  windows, SYSTEMATIC vs CHANCE verdict, and an audit-status line that blocks
  "100% fully audited" claims on data degradation.
- **gemini-planner:** Runs are only meaningful with pre-registered windows and a
  no-lookahead discipline; the plan should encode success bars before any run.

## Disagreement map
- Disagree on: none material (both sides agree the gate is pre-registered and
  falsification-first).
- Consensus on: chi-square regime gate, metric bundle, fixed windows, audit
  status, backtest-agent executor, archetype council advisory voices.

## Synthesis (hermes-bridge)
- Recommendation: APPROVE the v2 build as planned.
- Rationale: it converts the backtest registry from a dashboard into a decision
  ledger — every claim is tagged SYSTEMATIC or CHANCE and audited or degraded.
- Risk: the chi-square gate is low-power with sparse regime cells; the `fisher`
  fallback and the reported method mitigate but do not eliminate this.

## CEO ruling
> Approve. Build the v2 house backtest: chi-square regime gate, full metric

> with /backtest + history, and the archetype council. Install skills.sh skills

**Rationale:** A backtest that cannot distinguish systematic edge from chance is
