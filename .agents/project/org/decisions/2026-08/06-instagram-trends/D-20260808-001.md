## Synthesis (hermes-bridge, if T3)
- Recommendation: Hybrid heuristic size filter (<$10B) and discrete rolling Markov-Bayes with a dual-threshold floor and velocity inflection.
- Disagreements: Graph clustering and KDE are too complex/sparse for current data density.
- Rationale: High stability on alternative data while ensuring assets have minor signs of life (>=3 mentions, positive inflection).
- Risk: Micro-cap data sparsity.

## CEO ruling
> Use the hybrid reverse-heatmap approach: instead of the hottest being the most mentioned, scan around the ones with a very low heatmap using an arbitrary cutoff and then scan in the engine (MAD Robust Z-scores, Spiegelhalter funnels, >=3 mentions floor, and positive 7-day velocity inflection). Additionally, extract audio from Instagram Reel videos and transcribe it via Whisper to capture spoken ticker references.

**Rationale:** Capturing the transition of companies coming into stardom (low-mention baseline with positive velocity inflection) or highly under-valued outliers, while transcribing spoken audio from creators since critical tickers reside in speech, not just text blurbs.
