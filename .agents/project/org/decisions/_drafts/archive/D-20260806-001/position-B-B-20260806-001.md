- Blueprint redundancy: The blueprint already defines structured alt-data (Reddit, ApeWisdom, SEC new-filers). A deterministic feed from these achieves the discovery goal cleanly, avoiding video-trend noise and complex RNG mechanics.
RISKS:
- The 'off-pipeline' claim is contradictory: mandating a 6-run backtest ablation requires deep pipeline integration, risking scope creep and destabilization of the RM-FINAL baseline.
- Regression masquerading as success: The pre-registered bar allows metrics to actively degrade (e.g., lower Sharpe, higher turnover) as long as they stay above absolute historical floors, greenlighting a worse model.
