## 2. OOS validation (mandatory)

- **Risk minimizer**: covariance estimated on `date - 21d embargo` window, 252
  trailing trading days, never the full sample. `gradient_descent` returns
  `None` on <60 rows; `_estimate_cov` hard-requires >=60 rows.
- **Macro state**: `classify_state` uses a 90-day trailing median of BAA10Y and
  a 200-day trailing SMA of SPY; `classify_state_price` uses 200-day trailing
  SMAs of SPY and the HYG/LQD ratio. No full-window statistic is referenced.
- **Execution**: decisions are made at the calendar rebalance date from data
  strictly <= that date; orders execute at the first trading day strictly after.
  Purge+embargo discipline identical to D-20260803-001/002.
- **SGOV pre-inception (2020)**: covariance windows and targets exclude any
  ticker lacking a price at the decision date (`Portfolio._avail`), so no
  pre-2020 contamination from SGOV's NaN column.

## 4. Outcome summary (auditor note, not a recommendation)

- BASELINE SPY $31,700 (+217%), Sharpe 0.83, maxDD -33%.
- MACRO (Phase-1) $22,982 (+130%), Sharpe 1.38, maxDD -15%, 6 trades, fees
  $60.70 = 0.47% of gains, dividends $3,246 = **53x reallocation fees**.
- MINVAR $24,719 (+147%), Sharpe 1.43, maxDD -14%, 6 trades, fees $54 = 0.37%
  of gains, dividends $2,947 = **55x reallocation fees**.
- CEO hypothesis CONFIRMED: dividend income covers reallocation fees ~50x.
- Phase-1 de-risks (Sharpe 1.38-1.43 vs 0.83; maxDD halved) but trails
  buy-and-hold on raw return in a window dominated by US large-cap. Expected for
  a diversifying sleeve; risk-adjusted and drawdown outcomes are the test.

## 5. Caveats

1. FRED macro unreachable → macro signal is the price-proxy fallback; re-run +
   re-report when FRED returns.
2. Dividend yields are static annual estimates per fact sheets; monthly ex-div
   schedules would refine the coverage figure but not flip the ~50x margin.
3. Initial deployment is forced (documented rule: cash earns nothing).
