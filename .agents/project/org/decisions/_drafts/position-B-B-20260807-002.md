## Strongest disagreements vs the hybrid + cached-signal-adapter stance
1. **Cross-sectional corruption:** computing z-scores across cached
   traditional data and live IG data corrupts the baseline. You cannot compare
   an IG candidate's live alpha against a traditional megacap's cached alpha
   from months ago.
2. **The neutral-"hold" illusion:** a pass-through "hold" for missing
   qualitative data introduces severe coverage bias — it rewards assets for
   lacking data, destroying the qualitative gate's purpose and invalidating
   the comparison against traditional assets rigorously penalized by data.
